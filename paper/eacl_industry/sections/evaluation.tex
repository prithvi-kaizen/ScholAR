\section{Evaluation Design}
\paragraph{Retrospective corpus and tasks.}
The retrieval study uses 100 curated questions over 25 previously prepared scientific papers, with page-level gold evidence. Thirty answerable questions over 19 papers (10 each for mathematical, textual, and visual queries) test generation with and without post-generation repair. Twenty cross-paper swaps over 16 target papers probe responses when the requested fact was selected from a different source. A phrase-absence check passed for every swap, but semantic absence was not independently judged. These samples are not a paper-disjoint test set and should not be read as population estimates.

\paragraph{Comparisons and measurements.}
Retrieval compares keyword overlap, BM25, dense text, BM25+dense reciprocal-rank fusion (RRF), and the same fusion with a modality heuristic. A hit requires at least one gold page among distinct retrieved pages; the reported Hit@5 is not fractional recall over all gold pages. We also report mean reciprocal rank (MRR) and page nDCG@5. Answer comparisons share the exact raw completion and prompt within every paired case; only post-processing differs. Automatic token F1, reference-unit overlap F1, key-term coverage, and cited-page alignment are transparent proxies, not human factuality or citation-support judgments. Pipeline \textsc{Success} and \textsc{Abstained} describe execution outcomes, not the meaning of the final prose.

\paragraph{Post-hoc LLM-based answer evaluation.}
We also audit 150 answers from an earlier 200-question run over 10 papers, excluding the disjoint 50-case packet reserved for a planned human study. Exact question matching and a nonempty reference make 113 cases eligible for correctness review; 37 fail this gate but remain usable for grounding and citation checks against supplied excerpts. The batch workflow records correctness, completeness, evidence grounding, citation quality, and one failure type per case. Its retained rows identify the evaluator only as \texttt{manual-assistant-proxy}, although the planning file names a specific LLM, so we call this an LLM-based evaluation but do not make a named-model claim. We likewise exclude the visual-grounding distribution: its completed rows use a \texttt{partly\_supported} label absent from the frozen visual rubric. The remaining counts are post-hoc model judgments, not independent human annotations or a paper-disjoint test.

\paragraph{Human evaluation plan and current status.}
A private packet assigns the same 50 questions to five independent evaluators, for 250 planned ratings. The development-overlapping sample contains five questions from each of 10 papers; evaluators score correctness, completeness, grounding, citation quality, and utility on five-point scales, verify the supplied reference, and label every evidence item. At the September 21, 2026 manuscript freeze, the study database records zero submitted ratings across zero cases, so no human result is reported. After complete returns, the analysis slot will contain question-clustered bootstrap intervals, all 10 evaluator-pair agreement comparisons per dimension, reference-verification counts, evidence labels, and category and difficulty breakdowns.

\paragraph{Execution and uncertainty.}
The paired generation study used the same pinned Qwen 3.5 9B model~\citep{qwen2026qwen35}, quantized as Q4\_K\_M, on an 18\,GB M3 Pro in strict-local mode. Requests ran in terminal batches of at most five under a 50\% free-memory start gate; the service and model were unloaded between batches. The audit verifies all expected condition--case keys, dataset and model identities, identical raw paired completions and prompts, and separate errors and abstentions. A defective five-case control pilot was retained for audit but excluded; the corrected 20-case pairs were rerun. We use 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples clustered by paper for 95\% intervals. Latencies are wall-clock observations of these runs, not controlled warm-cache comparisons. The legacy 200-question result file used by the LLM-based evaluation does not retain an equivalent generator digest, so those verdicts are not merged with the paired intervention results. With these scopes fixed, we report retrieval first, then repair and controls, LLM judgments, human-study status, and operational latency.
