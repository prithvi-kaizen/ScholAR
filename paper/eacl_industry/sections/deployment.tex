\section{Operational Lessons}
\paragraph{Provenance must be separated from support.}
Source-scoped IDs make cited pages inspectable and prevent cross-document identifier collisions, but they do not establish that a page supports a claim. The low automatic answer overlap and missing gold-page labels for visual questions illustrate why citation validity, page alignment, and independent claim support require separate evaluation.

\paragraph{More components need not improve the system.}
The modest BM25+dense difference and the large loss from modality boosting challenge an ``add every channel'' design. The heuristic should remain disabled in a deployment configuration until a revised rule succeeds on independent documents. Likewise, deterministic repair is easy to audit but cannot be promoted as a safety mechanism from lexical overlap alone: the control statuses and page alignment move in the wrong direction.

\paragraph{Locality and latency are operational contracts.}
Strict-local analysis requires a prepared model and document bundle; acquisition and installation are separate network-enabled activities. The observed median request is tens of seconds, with a long first-use retrieval tail. This profile supports feasibility on the named workstation, not interactive responsiveness at scale. A deployment decision requires controlled cache conditions, peak memory, workload throughput, and actual researcher task outcomes.

\paragraph{Evaluation provenance is part of quality assurance.}
The LLM-based pass is useful because it exposes missing facts and weak evidence, but its model-identity and rubric mismatches prevent stronger claims. A deployable evaluation service should bind the judge provider, model version, prompt, schema, inputs, and outputs in the same trace and reject out-of-schema updates before aggregation. Completed rows are not enough when their provenance contract is incomplete, and they do not replace the pending human study. Together, these lessons frame the conclusion: auditability is an operational foundation, not a substitute for validated answer quality.
