\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Scientific-Document Question Answering.}
Specialized benchmarks such as QASPER \citep{dasigi2021qasper}, SciFact \citep{wadden2020scifact}, and M3SciQA \citep{li2024m3sciqa} have established that answering questions on scientific literature requires synthesizing complex multi-hop evidence across prose, equations, and experimental displays. However, benchmark evaluations typically assess isolated passage retrieval or free-form text metrics, providing little guidance on constructing interactive tools where users can audit cited evidence within the native document layout.

\paragraph{Citation-Grounded Document Assistants.}
Generating natural language with inline citations has received growing attention to mitigate hallucination \citep{gao2023alce,asai2024selfrag,verma2026reflectiverag}. In typical systems, citations point to whole text chunks or external document identifiers. In contrast, \textsc{SCHOLAR} grounds citations at the level of specific page-coordinate bounding boxes, allowing the user interface to visually pinpoint the exact text paragraph or figure crop supporting each claim.

\paragraph{Multimodal Document Understanding.}
Recent advances in multimodal document representation, such as ColPali \citep{faysse2025colpali} and multimodal scientific QA \citep{pramanick2024spiqa,wang2026arqa}, demonstrate the importance of visual layout in document comprehension. In a resource-constrained local deployment, full-page vision transformers can easily overwhelm consumer GPUs. \textsc{SCHOLAR} balances accuracy and resource consumption by combining layout-aware AST extraction \citep{shen2026ocr} with selective high-resolution visual cropping for figures and tables.

\paragraph{Auditable and Local Research Tools.}
While fine-grained factuality evaluators like FActScore \citep{min2023factscore} analyze atomic claim precision post-hoc, they generally rely on closed cloud APIs. Researchers working on unpublished manuscripts or sensitive proprietary drafts require strict local data boundaries. \textsc{SCHOLAR} fills this operational gap by executing retrieval, generation, and deterministic verification entirely on consumer workstations, recording a transparent audit graph for every query.
